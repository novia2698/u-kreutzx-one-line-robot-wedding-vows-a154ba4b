One line of wedding vows for a robot couple: a promise to have and to hold through sickness and low battery, until a factory reset do us part.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the vow to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).

## The vow

> I take you to be my lawfully wedded co-processor, to have and to hold, in sickness and in low battery, until a factory reset do us part.

It keeps the classic wedding-vow shape — "to have and to hold", "until ... do us part" — and lands the laugh in the last few words, swapping death for the robot equivalent: a factory reset. 136 characters, one line.
